RIX PRIVACY POLICY

Effective 2026-11-03.

Rix is a game for iOS and Android. It collects no personal data. It sends no data to anyone.

WHAT RIX DOES NOT DO

  • Rix has no account and no sign-in.
  • Rix shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.
  • Rix has no analytics and no tracking.
  • Rix does not connect to the internet.
  • Rix asks for no permissions.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

Rix keeps one save file on your phone: your best score and level, your settings, the skins you unlocked, the tips you saw and the number of runs you played. It never leaves your phone, except in your phone's own backup if you turned that on. When you delete Rix, the file goes with it.

SHARING

When you tap SHARE after a run, Rix gives your phone's share sheet a short text (score, level and seed) and a picture of the game at the end of the run. Rix sends nothing itself. The app you choose in the share sheet handles what you send under its own policy.

CHILDREN

Rix collects no data from anyone, children included.

CHANGES

A change to this policy appears on this page with a new date.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/yonson2/rix/issues.
