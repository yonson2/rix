RIX

DRAW A LINE
CLAIM THE FIELD

Rix is a neon arcade game for iPhone and Android, inspired by Qix. Draw a line from wall to wall with one finger: the side without enemies becomes yours. An enemy that touches your line while you draw it ends the run. Claim 75% of the field to clear the level. Power-ups help, new enemies join, and the points double with each level.

Free, with no ads and no purchases.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.
