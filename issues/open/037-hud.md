## Problem
The HUD is three text lines laid out with wall-thickness constants (`ui.rs`): "Level: N",
the score, "NN/75%". It is small on phones and gives no sense of progress toward the
target. todo.md: "improve ui on displayed level/points/progress".

## Approach
A progress bar toward the target, score with a count-up animation on gains, level badge,
pause button ([034]); laid out against the logical playfield ([019]) and the safe area
([023]).

## Verification
`just shot` on 390x844 and 625x625 shows a readable HUD that does not overlap the field.
