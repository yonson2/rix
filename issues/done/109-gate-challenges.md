## Problem
M9 (challenges, D39) has choices that only Pedro makes. The answers shape [120] to [125].

## Approach
Pedro answers the questions below. The answers go into `decisions.md`, the issues they
touch get a note, and this gate closes. A recommendation follows each question.

1. **Daily seed:** yes or no? Pedro asked "why?" (2026-11-05). The reasons: every player
   gets the same field that day, so players can compare and talk about it; it gives a
   reason to open the game each day (a count of days in a row); it needs no server (the
   date makes the seed); and a daily board gives every player a new chance each day, while
   the all-time board fills up and stops moving. [012] Q4 said no daily seed for v1 only.
   Recommended: yes, as a daily challenge (the day's seed with one of the curated rule
   sets, in turn).
2. **A death in a countdown challenge:** it ends the run, or it costs 10 seconds and the
   level goes on? Recommended: it costs 10 seconds.
3. **What a challenge run counts toward:** the lifetime stats and the games played, the
   medals, the best level (and its skin unlocks), the best score and the main leaderboard?
   Recommended: stats, games and medals yes; best level, best score and the main board no
   (a "start at level 10" challenge would open every level skin at once).
4. **Locks:** all challenges open from the start, or some open with games played? A
   shared code always plays. Recommended: all open.
5. **Boards:** a leaderboard per curated challenge (one ID per challenge in each store,
   owner-run), or local bests only? Recommended: local bests, plus one daily board if Q1
   is yes.
6. **The first list** in [123]: keep it, change it, add to it?
7. **M6 (web):** before M7, between two of M7 to M9, or after M9?
