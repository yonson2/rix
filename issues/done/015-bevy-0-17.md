## Problem
Second step of D3. Bevy 0.17 splits buffered events from observer events and moves render
crates; avian 0.4 follows it.

## Approach
Bump `bevy` to the latest 0.17.x and `avian2d` to the latest 0.4.x. Apply
https://bevy.org/learn/migration-guides/0-16-to-0-17/ and avian's 0.4 changes; checklist in
Notes. Only what the step needs.

## Verification
`cargo build`, `cargo clippy --all-targets` (no new warnings) and `cargo test` pass;
`just shot` and `just shot-menu` match the previous step; a manual run draws, claims, dies
and restarts.
