## Full description (Play, 4000 characters at most)

Rix is a fast arcade game for one finger.

Draw a line from wall to wall. When it closes, the side without enemies becomes yours. Claim 75% of the field to clear the level. If both sides hold enemies, one side is claimed anyway and the enemies inside are eaten. But if an enemy touches your line while you draw it, the run is over.

KNOW YOUR ENEMIES
• Ball: bounces off the walls.
• Square: fades out and jumps away.
• Arrow: follows your finger.
• Splitter: kill it and two more hatch.
• Shooter: fires at a slow line.

GRAB POWER-UPS
Claim an item to use it:
• Bomb: one enemy blows up.
• Gem: double points for 10 seconds.
• Hourglass: enemies stop for 4 seconds.
• Auto-line: your line flies to a wall.
• Star: your line kills for 6 seconds.

ENDLESS LEVELS
Each level brings more enemies, and points double every level. One death ends the run, so every line counts.

UNLOCK NEW LINES AND WALLS
Reach levels 5, 10, 15 and 20 to unlock the laser and chain lines and the brick and circuit walls.

SHARE YOUR SEED
Every run has a seed. Share it with your score when the run ends, or type a seed to start from the same field and enemies as a friend.

MADE FOR PHONES
• One-finger controls and short runs.
• A neon look with glow and a retro CRT screen.
• A short tutorial on your first run.
• Settings for music, sound, screen shake, haptics and flashing effects.

FREE AND OPEN
No ads. No in-app purchases. No data collected. Rix is free software under the GNU GPL 3.0, and its source code is public on GitHub.
